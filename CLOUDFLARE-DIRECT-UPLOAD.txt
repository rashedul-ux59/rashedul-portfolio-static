IMPORTANT: Cloudflare Direct Upload needs the built website, not the source-code ZIP.

1) Install Node.js 22+ on a computer.
2) Extract this project ZIP.
3) Double-click `Build-For-Cloudflare.bat`.
4) Wait until it creates `Cloudflare-Upload.zip`.
5) Upload that generated ZIP in Cloudflare → Workers & Pages → Create application → Pages → Direct Upload.

Do not use Workers Builds, Wrangler, or OpenNext.
